Getting started+

Your dashboard shows the features available for your account.

  • Patron: view your care schedule, appointments and personal wellbeing features.
  • Patron Admin: organise care and manage the people available under your plan.
  • Caretaker: use your assigned care features and check the relevant schedules.

Tap a dashboard card to open a feature. Use its Home button to return to the dashboard.

You can open this page using Help in your dashboard header or Help & Videos in the menu.

Medicines & reminders+

Everyday example: a family member helps keep a Patron's prescribed medicine schedule organised.

  1. The Patron Admin opens Schedule Medicines and enters the prescribed schedule.
  2. The Patron checks View Medicine. Patron Admins can use View Medicine Schedule.
  3. For reminder settings, open Medicine Reminder Notifications in the menu and follow the instructions shown.

Reminder delivery depends on your phone settings and permissions. Keep medicine details consistent with the prescription given by your clinician.

Doctor appointments+

Everyday example: a family member keeps the details of an upcoming doctor visit in one place.

  1. The Patron Admin opens Schedule Doctor Appointment.
  2. Enter the appointment details and check them before saving.
  3. Open View Doctor Appointment to review the saved details.

Recording a visit in ELDARA does not itself book or confirm an appointment with the clinic. Confirm arrangements with the clinic separately.

Daily wellness+

Everyday example: a Patron records how they are feeling so they can look back at their recent wellbeing.

  1. Open Wellness on your dashboard.
  2. Follow the check-in questions about sleep, mood and discomfort.
  3. Use 7-day history to review recent check-ins.

A wellbeing entry describes how you feel. It is not a medical diagnosis.

My Walking+

Everyday example: a Patron checks their walking activity while carrying their phone.

  1. Open My Walking on your dashboard.
  2. Follow the setup or permission instructions shown in your installed app.
  3. Carry your phone while walking and check your steps and estimated distance.

The available controls depend on your app version. If your version offers Past 7 days, tap it to view recent daily totals.

Step counting needs a supported physical phone. Distance is an estimate. Your walking activity is personal and is not shared with family or caretakers.

Connecting with caretakers+

Everyday example: a Patron Admin looks for support with a Patron's everyday care.

  1. Open Manage Caretaker.
  2. Use Find caretakers to review available profiles.
  3. Use Private inbox to discuss arrangements and Assignments to review them.

Check the agreed dates, times and responsibilities with the caretaker. A listed profile alone does not confirm a care assignment.
