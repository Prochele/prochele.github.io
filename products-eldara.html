ELDARA elderly care and wellness app

ELDARA is Prochele's elderly care app for India, designed to help families, patrons, and caretakers coordinate everyday care for elderly people, people living with dementia, and adults of all ages.

Elder care, simplified

Support everyday care with a medicine scheduler, medicine reminders, doctor appointments, daily activity reminders, vitals monitoring through manually entered records and reports, wellness check-ins, a personal step counter, and caretaker connections.

Available features

Daily wellness

  • Record wellbeing, sleep, mood and discomfort, and review a 7-day history.
  • Keep assistance requests for daily support and appointments within the app.
  • Authorised care roles can view Wellness; caretakers see only their currently assigned patrons.

My walking and step counter

  • Use the step counter during a walk on a supported phone, or add a manual walk.
  • View steps, distance, active time, personal goals and a 7-day history.
  • Walking records stay personal to the account and are not shared with family or caretakers.
  • Phone tracking pauses when you leave the walking screen, lock the phone or background the app.

Caretaker connections

  • Find registered caretakers and review profiles, working hours, availability and reviews.
  • Send a contact request and use a private inbox after acceptance.
  • Propose dated care assignments for the caretaker to confirm, and keep assignment history.

People and care roles

  • Manage patrons and caretakers.
  • Assign caretaker responsibilities.
  • Support family care coordination.

Medicine scheduler and reminders

  • Schedule medicines with dosage, timing, dates, and intake notes.
  • View daily medicine schedules.
  • Mark medicines as taken or not taken.
  • Use medicine reminders and daily activity reminders for routine care tasks.

Doctor appointments

  • Schedule and view doctor appointments.
  • Record doctor, clinic, specialty, location, phone, purpose, status, and feedback.

Vitals monitoring and reports

  • Record vitals manually, including blood pressure, temperature, pulse, sugar, oxygen, heart rate, and respiration.
  • Keep track of recorded readings; ELDARA does not continuously measure these vitals.
  • View trend reports such as blood pressure charts.

Health spotlight and support

  • Display health insights and support information.
  • Provide access to emergency, support, and notification actions.

Facial health scan

  • Camera-based contactless wellness estimate.
  • Includes medical disclaimer language for responsible use.
