Healthcare App: ELDARA

ELDARA is Prochele's elderly care app for India, designed to help families, patrons, and caretakers coordinate everyday care for elderly people, people living with dementia, and adults of all ages.

Elder care, simplified

ELDARA brings care tasks into one mobile experience: people management, medicine scheduling, doctor appointments, daily reminders, vitals, reports, emergency support, notifications, and contactless wellness estimates.

Medicine scheduler Medicine reminders Track vitals Doctor appointments Reports Caretaker assignment Daily reminders Emergency support Notifications Facial health scan

Available features

People and care roles

  • Manage patrons and caretakers.
  • Assign caretaker responsibilities.
  • Support family care coordination.

Medicine and reminders

  • Schedule medicines with dosage, timing, dates, and intake notes.
  • View daily medicine schedules.
  • Mark medicines as taken or not taken.
  • Use daily reminders for routine care tasks.

Doctor appointments

  • Schedule and view doctor appointments.
  • Record doctor, clinic, specialty, location, phone, purpose, status, and feedback.

Vitals and reports

  • Record vitals manually, including blood pressure, temperature, pulse, sugar, oxygen, heart rate, and respiration.
  • View trend reports such as blood pressure charts.

Health spotlight and support

  • Display health insights and support information.
  • Provide access to emergency, support, and notification actions.

Facial health scan

  • Camera-based contactless wellness estimate.
  • Includes medical disclaimer language for responsible use.
