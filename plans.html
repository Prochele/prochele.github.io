Role Definitions

  • PTA (Patron Admin) – Full control over PT profiles and caretaker assignment.
  • PT (Patron) – Can view own medical data and mark medicine as taken.
  • CT (Caretaker) – Can operate only on assigned PT. Cannot modify prescriptions.
