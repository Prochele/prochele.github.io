ELDARA Subscription Plans

Basic (Self)
Designed for individual users managing their own health independently. One mobile login with essential medicine and vitals tracking.
  • 1 PT (Patron) Profile
  • 1 Mobile Login
  • Add & Schedule Medicines
  • Add Appointments
  • Add Vitals
  • Add Daily Activities
  • Add 1 CT (Caretaker)
Standard (Self & Family)
Perfect for small families where one PTA manages self and one family member. Dual mobile access with advanced adherence tracking.
  • 2 PT (Patron) Profiles
  • 2 Mobile Logins
  • Advanced Medicine Adherence
  • Add 2 CT (Caretakers)
  • Monthly Health Summary
Premium (Family)
Built for large families or NRIs managing multiple dependents. Includes reports, alerts, and priority support.
  • Up to 5 PT (Patron) Profiles
  • Multiple Mobile Logins
  • Weekly Health Reports
  • Emergency Alert Feature
  • Priority Support
  • Multiple CT (Caretakers)
Caretaker Pro
For professional caregivers assisting assigned patients. Requires KYC and allows operational logging only.
  • KYC Mandatory
  • Operate Assigned PT Only
  • Log Medicine Administration
  • Add Vitals
  • Add Appointment Logs
  • Add Daily Activities

Role Definitions

PTA – Patron Admin
The primary account holder who manages PT profiles, medicines, appointments, and assigns CTs within plan limits.
PT – Patron
A patient profile under a PTA account. Can view personal data and mark medicines as taken.
CT – Caretaker
A verified caregiver assigned by PTA. Can log vitals and activities but cannot modify prescriptions.
