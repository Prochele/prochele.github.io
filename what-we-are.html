What we are

Prochele Software Solutions Private Limited is a startup building software products and services for care, compliance, and business operations.

A startup focused on elderly care and business software

Our flagship product, ELDARA, is an elderly care app developed for families in India, including support for elderly people living with dementia and adults of all ages who need organized care coordination.

Care coordination

ELDARA supports medicine schedules, doctor appointments, daily reminders, caretaker assignment, emergency access, and family support workflows.

Health awareness

The app helps users record and view vitals, reports, and wellness estimates, while keeping appropriate medical disclaimers clear.

Business solutions

Beyond healthcare, Prochele develops software that improves business processes, monitoring, documentation, and digital service delivery.
